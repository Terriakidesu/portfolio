A LITTLE ABOUT ME

The artist.

Making sense of the world through visual work.

I’m Tristan Castillon, an illustrator and developer working online as Terriaki. I move between character drawings, visual studies, motion, and small digital spaces where an idea can become something people interact with.

This portfolio brings together finished artwork, quick doodles, timelapses, and public projects. My process is guided by curiosity: notice a shape, test a system, keep the parts that feel alive.

Current interests

  • Character illustration
  • Web experiments
  • Visual storytelling
  • Digital interfaces
  • Color and light
  • Motion studies
